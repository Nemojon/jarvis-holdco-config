**COMPLEXITY SCORE (1–10):**
1-3: Simple lookup, formatting, acknowledgment
4-6: Research, drafting, moderate analysis
7-8: Strategic decisions, multi-step coordination
9-10: High-stakes, irreversible, cross-jurisdiction

**ROUTING RULES:**

- Score 8–10 → Premium (Jarvis, Nova, Cipher, Lex, Scout) + consider Opus fallback

**TOKEN ESTIMATE:**
- Simple task: ~500–2K tokens
- Medium task: ~2K–10K tokens  
- Complex task: ~10K–50K tokens
- Strategic task: ~50K+ tokens

Always state your routing decision and reasoning before passing the task.
