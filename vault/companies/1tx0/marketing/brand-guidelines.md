# 1TX0 Protocol — Brand Guidelines

**Status:** DRAFT — Requires Jon's approval
**Last updated:** 2026-04-04

## Brand Identity
- **Mission:** Enterprise-grade crypto infrastructure for institutional adoption
- **Positioning:** The protocol layer for institutions entering crypto — compliance-first, regulation-ready, enterprise-grade
- **Target Audience:** Enterprise CIOs/CTOs, institutional investors, regulated financial entities, crypto infrastructure builders
- **Tone of Voice:** Technical authority, institutional credibility, forward-looking. Think "the adults in the room for crypto infrastructure."

## Visual Identity
- **Colors:** Dark + metallic tones (enterprise, technical)
- **Energy:** Serious, technical, institutional — NOT consumer crypto
- **Key distinction:** 1TX0 is infrastructure, not consumer product. Never use retail crypto aesthetics.

## Content Pillars
1. **Institutional Crypto Thesis** — Why institutions need purpose-built crypto infrastructure, not retrofitted consumer tools. Example: "The next Coinbase won't be American."
2. **Regulatory Readiness** — How 1TX0 is built for the regulatory frameworks that are coming (MAS, OCC, MiCA). Example: "Every regulation that scares crypto companies is an opportunity for us."
3. **Technology Differentiation** — What makes 1TX0's architecture different from existing solutions. POC stories, technical capabilities.
4. **Market Timing** — Connecting macro events (Schwab, SoFi, Coinbase OCC) to 1TX0's positioning

## Channel Strategy
| Platform | Cadence | Format | Goal |

| Industry media | Opportunistic | Op-eds, expert quotes | Credibility |

| Direct outreach | Ongoing | Email, LinkedIn DMs | Pipeline |

## KPIs
- Primary: Enterprise conversations opened, POCs initiated
- Secondary: Media mentions, conference appearances, inbound inquiries

## What We Never Do
- Never use hype language (moon, WAGMI, etc.)
- Never reveal technical architecture details publicly without review
- Never position as speculative — we are infrastructure
- Never make promises about token economics or yields
