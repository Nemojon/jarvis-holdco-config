# BSQ — Brand Guidelines

**Status:** DRAFT — Requires Jon's approval
**Last updated:** 2026-04-04

## Brand Identity
- **Mission:** Premium creative agency delivering measurable growth for ambitious brands
- **Positioning:** Full-service creative and growth agency — strategy, content, social media management, and lead gen for founders and companies who want to scale their presence
- **Target Audience:** Founders, CEOs, personal brands, B2B companies needing social media management, content creation, and lead generation
- **Tone of Voice:** Creative, energetic, results-driven. Think "your growth-obsessed creative partner." Not corporate. Not generic.

## Visual Identity
- **Colors:** Bold, modern palette (to be defined by Luna + Echo)
- **Energy:** Creative, dynamic, premium but approachable
- **Key distinction:** BSQ is a service business — marketing should showcase client results, not just our own content

## Content Pillars
1. **Client Results** — Before/after engagement metrics, growth stories, case studies. Example: "This founder went from 200 to 12K LinkedIn followers in 90 days. Here's the playbook."
2. **Growth Tactics** — Actionable social media and content insights. Example: "The first line of your LinkedIn post gets 80% of the decision. Here's how to nail it every time."
3. **Behind the Agency** — How BSQ operates, team culture, creative process. Example: "Our AI-powered creative workflow produces 13 pieces of content per week per client. Here's how."
4. **Founder Brand Building** — Why personal branding matters, how to start, common mistakes. Example: "Your company page gets 2% of the reach your personal profile does. Stop hiding behind a logo."

## Channel Strategy
| Platform | Cadence | Format | Goal |
|----------|---------|--------|------|
| LinkedIn | 3x/week | Text posts, carousels | Lead gen, credibility |
| Instagram | 2x/week | Reels, stories | Brand awareness |
| TikTok | 2x/week | Short-form video | Reach, virality |
| Email | 1x/week | Case studies, tips | Nurture inbound |

## KPIs
- Primary: Inbound briefs/month, retainer value
- Secondary: LinkedIn engagement, follower growth, content reach, client satisfaction scores

## What We Never Do
- Never share client data without permission
- Never promise specific follower counts or virality
- Never use templated, generic content — every piece is custom
- Never let a client profile go dark 2+ days without alert
