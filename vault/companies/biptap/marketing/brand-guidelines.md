# Biptap — Brand Guidelines

**Status:** DRAFT — Requires Jon's approval
**Last updated:** 2026-04-04

**Note:** Biptap is the parent entity / technology brand for AbsolutePay. Marketing efforts are primarily under the AbsolutePay brand for client-facing communications. Biptap is used for investor communications and strategic positioning.

## Brand Identity
- **Mission:** Building the financial infrastructure layer for emerging markets
- **Positioning:** The regulated crypto-fiat infrastructure company — what Coinbase is doing for the US, Biptap is building for the rest of the world
- **Target Audience:** Investors, strategic partners, enterprise clients, fintech ecosystem players
- **Tone of Voice:** Visionary, authoritative, data-driven. Think "CEO addressing the future of finance."

## Visual Identity
- **Colors:** Black + gold (premium, institutional)
- **Energy:** Institutional, forward-looking, bold
- Shared visual language with AbsolutePay but elevated for investor/strategic context

## Content Pillars
1. **Regulated Crypto Banking Vision** — The thesis: regulated fiat+crypto infrastructure for non-US markets. "While the US figures out how to add crypto to what already exists, we're building financial infrastructure for markets that never had a bank."
2. **Market Opportunity** — GCC, Africa, SEA market sizing, regulatory landscape, first-mover advantage
3. **Institutional Validation** — When Schwab/SoFi/Coinbase moves validate our thesis. "Coinbase just proved regulators are ready. So are we."
4. **Founder Journey** — Jon's personal narrative as the connecting tissue

## Channel Strategy
| Platform | Cadence | Format | Goal |
|----------|---------|--------|------|
| Investor updates | Monthly | Email + PDF | Investor relations |
| LinkedIn (Jon's) | Weaved into personal brand | Narrative posts | Positioning |
| Media / PR | Opportunistic | Op-eds, quotes | Credibility |

## KPIs
- Primary: Investor conversations opened, pipeline value
- Secondary: Media mentions, speaking invitations, partner inquiries

## What We Never Do
- Never over-promise on regulatory status
- Never publish financials publicly
- Never position as competing with US players — we complement, we don't compete
