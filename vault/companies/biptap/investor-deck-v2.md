## Slide 2: The Problem

### Businesses in Emerging Markets Lose 3-7% of Revenue to Payment Friction

**The Pain Points:**

1. **Fragmented Infrastructure**
   - Average business uses 4+ payment providers
   - No unified dashboard or reconciliation
   - Integration takes 3-6 months per provider

2. **Excessive Fees**
   - Cross-border transactions: 4-7% total cost
   - Currency conversion markups: 2-3%
   - Hidden fees buried in fine print

3. **Slow Settlement**
   - Domestic: 2-3 business days
   - Cross-border: 5-7 business days
   - Cash flow nightmare for SMEs

4. **Poor Developer Experience**
   - Outdated APIs (SOAP, batch files)
   - Limited documentation
   - No sandbox environments

**Market Reality:**
> "We spent 6 months integrating three payment providers. Two of them still randomly fail. Our finance team spends 20 hours/week on reconciliation." — CFO, Mid-market E-commerce Company
