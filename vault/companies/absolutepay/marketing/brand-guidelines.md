# AbsolutePay / Biptap — Brand Guidelines

**Status:** DRAFT — Requires Jon's approval
**Last updated:** 2026-04-04

## Brand Identity
- **Mission:** Making global payments infrastructure accessible to every SME and fintech
- **Positioning:** White-label payment infrastructure for fintechs, neobanks, and SMEs — fast integration, full compliance, global reach
- **Target Audience:** SME founders, fintech CTOs, neobank operators, payment companies seeking white-label solutions
- **Tone of Voice:** Professional, confident, technically credible, globally-minded. Not stuffy. Think "fintech builder talking to fintech builders."

## Visual Identity
- **Colors:** Dark navy + gold accents (premium fintech aesthetic)
- **Energy:** Professional, trustworthy, modern
- **Logo treatment:** Always use approved logo. Never distort.

## Content Pillars
1. **Infrastructure Expertise** — How payment rails work, compliance nuances, integration patterns. Example hooks: "Most fintechs don't fail on product. They fail on payments infrastructure."
2. **Global Expansion Stories** — Market entry insights for GCC, Africa, SEA. Example hooks: "We launched in 3 new markets last quarter. Here's what nobody tells you about cross-border compliance."
3. **Builder Credibility** — Behind-the-scenes of running payment infra. Example hooks: "Running a payment company means you're always one regulatory change away from rethinking everything."
4. **Client Success** — Anonymized wins, integration milestones, pipeline momentum. Example hooks: "One of our clients went from zero to 10K transactions/month in 90 days. Here's how."

## Channel Strategy
| Platform | Cadence | Format | Goal |
|----------|---------|--------|------|
| LinkedIn (Jon's) | 2x/week | Text posts, case studies | Thought leadership, lead gen |
| LinkedIn (Company) | 1x/week | Industry insights | Brand awareness |
| Email newsletter | 1x/month | Industry digest | Nurture pipeline |

## KPIs
- Primary: SME leads/week, waitlist signups
- Secondary: Partner demo requests, press mentions, LinkedIn engagement rate

## What We Never Do
- Never claim features we don't have live
- Never name client companies without permission
- Never discuss regulatory issues publicly
- Never use crypto-bro tone — we are infrastructure, not speculation
