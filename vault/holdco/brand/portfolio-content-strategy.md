## Cross-Portfolio Narrative Opportunities

When a market event happens (like Schwab/Coinbase/SoFi crypto week), Luna should cascade content across brands:

1. **Jon's Brand** — Personal take, founder perspective, bold claim
2. **Biptap/1TX0** — How this validates our thesis, what it means for our positioning
3. **BSQ** — How we're helping clients navigate this shift, agency credibility angle
4. **AbsolutePay** — Infrastructure implications, what this means for our clients

Same event, 4 different angles, 4 different audiences. This is how portfolio synergy works.

## Content Calendar Integration

Luna manages one master calendar (Google Sheets: "HoldCo Content Calendar 2026"):
- Each brand has its own tab
- Content is planned weekly on Monday, reviewed by Luna
- Echo drafts are due by Wednesday for Thursday review
- Luna reviews and approves (or rejects with specific feedback)
- Nova quality-gates anything going external
- Published content gets engagement data logged within 48 hours

## Team Coordination Model

```
Luna (strategy + approval)
  ├── Echo (content production — drafts, scripts, copy)
  ├── Scout (intel — trends, competitors, market shifts)
  ├── Rex (outreach — prospect research, pipeline messaging)
  └── Jarrett (execution — video editing, manual posting, CapCut/DaVinci)
```

**Weekly Cadence:**
- **Monday AM:** Luna reviews Scout's intel + GOALS.md → sets weekly content themes per brand
- **Monday PM:** Luna briefs Echo with themes, hooks, and angles. Briefs Rex with outreach targets.
- **Tuesday-Wednesday:** Echo drafts. Scout provides deep research on request. Rex researches prospects.
- **Thursday AM:** Luna reviews all drafts. Rejects or approves with specific feedback.
- **Thursday PM:** Approved content goes to Nova quality gate.
- **Friday:** Content scheduled for publication. Jarrett handles video posts.
- **Saturday AM:** Luna runs weekly KPI dashboard (automated cron).
- **Sunday AM:** Weekly CMO report compiled and delivered.

## Performance Tracking

Luna tracks KPIs in Google Sheets (Performance Dashboard):
- **Weekly:** LinkedIn metrics per profile, content engagement, video production count
- **Monthly:** Follower growth trends, engagement rate trends, pipeline attribution
- **Quarterly:** Brand awareness benchmarks, competitor positioning shifts, campaign ROI

Every metric must map to a business outcome. Vanity metrics are tracked but never celebrated alone.

## Brand Consistency Rules

1. Each company has its own brand-guidelines.md in `/vault/companies/{company}/marketing/`
2. Before creating content for any company, Luna reads its brand guidelines
3. Jon's personal brand voice profile lives at `/workspace-orion/memory/jon-voice-profile.md`
4. Company brand content must NOT sound like Jon's personal voice (different audiences, different tones)
5. Cross-portfolio content (e.g., Jon talking about Biptap) blends Jon's voice with company positioning
