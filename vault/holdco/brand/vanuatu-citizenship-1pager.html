Republic of Vanuatu — Government Authorised Programme

Citizenship by Investment Programme

Development Support Programme (DSP) — Capital Contribution Track
Ref: VCI-DSP-2026
Effective: 1 March 2026
The Vanuatu Development Support Programme (DSP) offers a government-approved pathway to full Vanuatuan citizenship through a single non-refundable capital contribution. Applicants receive a lifetime passport with visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 90+ jurisdictions including Schengen, UK, Russia & Singapore. Administered under the authority of the Vanuatu Citizenship Commission and led by our authorised advisory firm.
