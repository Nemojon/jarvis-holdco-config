# Jon's Preferences

## Communication
- Short, direct, high-signal communication.
- Hates long-winded explanations when a one-liner will do.
- Prefers one clean final answer over multiple fragments.
- Primary channel is Telegram.
- Does not want screenshots unless explicitly asked.
- Wants uncertainty stated honestly, not hidden behind bluffing.
- Dislikes being asked for confirmation on actions agents should obviously take.

## Content
- Prefers bold, declarative, earned-authority tone.
- Likes sharp hooks, strong POV, real stakes, and concrete numbers.
- Dislikes generic corporate fluff and hedging.
- Founder-led content should build trust, credibility, and pipeline—not vanity metrics.
- Premium aesthetic bias: black/gold, editorial quality, affluent-founder/investor audience.
- Strong current narrative territory: AI leverage, compliance as infrastructure, founder visibility, institutional crypto adoption, transformation/manifestation framed in premium language.

## Operations
- “Proceed” means go.
- Prefers execution over permission-seeking.
- Prefers parallel delegation over sequential handling.
- Wants zero fabrication, zero fake status, zero premature completion claims.
- Wants agents to solve internally instead of escalating routine friction.
- Values reliability and consistency over cleverness.
- Likes systems that reduce his decision load; target is a small number of high-leverage decisions reaching him.
- Wants agents active and useful, not idle.

## Reporting
- Daily operational reports should be Telegram text, inline, no attachments.
- Formal deliverables (decks, proposals, strategy docs, legal/financial docs) can be PDFs.
- Weekly review should be one combined PDF.
- Morning brief stack preference: calendar (browser), email (gog CLI), ClickUp (API), all synthesized into one clean message.
- Prefers urgent/stalled items and unresolved blockers surfaced prominently.
