# Legal & Compliance

## When to Use
Contract review, regulatory monitoring, AML/sanctions checks, compliance assessments, legal document drafting.

## Tools Required
- Google Drive MCP (contracts, templates): readGoogleDoc, getGoogleDocContent, createGoogleDoc, search
- Notion MCP (compliance tracker): notion-search, notion-fetch, notion-create-database
- Gmail MCP (legal correspondence): gmail_search_messages, gmail_read_message, gmail_create_draft
- ClickUp (legal review tracking): clickup_filter_tasks, clickup_create_task
- Browser for regulatory portals:
  - CBUAE (Central Bank UAE): https://www.centralbank.ae
  - RBI (Reserve Bank India): https://www.rbi.org.in
  - VARA (Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority): https://www.vara.ae
  - FATF (Financial Action Task Force): https://www.fatf-gafi.org

## Critical Escalation Protocol
**FATF/AML/sanctions issues bypass the normal agent chain — reach Jon immediately.**
This is the ONLY exception to the "no direct pings to Jon" rule.

## Rules
- Never guess on legal matters — research or flag as uncertain
- Always flag risk, even if it delays execution
- VAULT first — never redraft what exists as template
- Nova reviews legal strategy before Jon sees it
- Zero tolerance for missed compliance issues — false positives are OK, missed issues are NOT
- No screenshots (COR-001)
