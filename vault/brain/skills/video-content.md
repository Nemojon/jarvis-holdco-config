## 2. Tool Assignments

| Tool | Best For | When to Use |
|------|----------|-------------|
| **Descript** | Talking head, podcasts, interviews | Jon records himself talking → edit by editing the transcript. Auto-removes filler words, fixes eye contact, adds captions. PRIMARY tool for founder content. |
| **CapCut Pro** | Social cuts, trending formats, Reels/TikTok | Fast social edits, animated captions, trending templates, B-roll overlays. PRIMARY tool for short-form distribution. |
| **DaVinci Resolve** | Professional edits, color grading, sizzle reels | High-production investor content, TEDx highlight reel, brand films. Use when quality ceiling matters more than speed. |
| **Canva** | Thumbnails, graphics, social cards, intros/outros | Quick branded graphics, video thumbnails per platform, lower-thirds templates. Already MCP-integrated. |
| **Midjourney** | Premium AI visuals, hero images | Brand imagery for video backgrounds, conceptual visuals, LinkedIn carousel graphics. When stock isn't good enough. |
| **Gamma** | Presentation-style videos, pitch content | Investor decks as video walkthroughs, product demos with slides. Already MCP-integrated. |
| **Opus Clip** | Long-form → short-form repurposing | Feed a 10+ min video, get 5-10 viral-scored short clips auto-generated. Use after any long recording session. |

### Decision Tree
```
Recording Jon talking? → Descript
Making it social-ready? → CapCut
Need it polished/cinematic? → DaVinci Resolve
Need a thumbnail? → Canva
Need premium brand visual? → Midjourney
Turning a presentation into video? → Gamma
Have long content, need shorts? → Opus Clip
```

## 3. Production Pipeline

### Phase 1: Pre-Production (Echo + Luna)

**Scripting (Echo owns):**
- Read jon-voice-profile.md before writing any script
- Scripts follow the brand voice for the target company (see Echo soul.md)
- Format: HOOK (first 3 seconds) → BODY (value delivery) → CTA (clear next step)
- Every script has a working title, target platform, and estimated duration
- Save to: workspace-orion/memory/video-scripts-{date}.md

**Planning (Luna owns):**
- Check trending formats weekly (Scout assists with social intel)
- Maintain a content calendar integrating video with Echo's LinkedIn text posts
- Shot list for any multi-camera or B-roll shoots
- Brand guidelines per company (reference VAULT/{company}/marketing/)

### Phase 2: Recording

**Minimum Setup for Jon:**
- iPhone 15/16 Pro (4K, Cinematic Mode) or webcam with good lighting
- Ring light or window light (natural > artificial)
- Lapel mic or AirPods Pro (audio quality > video quality for talking head)
- Clean background — bookshelf, office, or branded backdrop
- Record in Descript (desktop) for auto-transcription, or phone for mobility

**Recording Rules:**
- Batch record: 3-5 videos in one session (saves setup time)
- Look at camera, not screen (Descript can fix eye contact in post)
- Keep raw takes — Descript edits by transcript, so mistakes are just deleted text
- Minimum 1080p. Prefer 4K for cropping flexibility.

### Phase 3: Post-Production

| Step | Tool | Who |
|------|------|-----|
| Rough cut + filler removal | Descript | Luna/Jarrett |
| Caption generation | Descript (export SRT) or CapCut (animated captions) | Luna/Jarrett |
| Social formatting (9:16, 1:1, 16:9) | CapCut | Luna/Jarrett |
| Color grading (premium content only) | DaVinci Resolve | Jarrett |
| Thumbnail creation | Canva (branded templates) | Luna |
| Brand intro/outro | Canva (animated) or CapCut (template) | Luna |
| Short-form repurposing | Opus Clip → CapCut polish | Luna |

### Phase 4: Review & Approval

```
Echo drafts script → Luna produces video → Nova reviews (quality gate) → Kishin + Amina approve → Publish
```

**Quality Gate Checklist (Nova):**
- [ ] Hook grabs attention in first 3 seconds
- [ ] Brand voice matches target company
- [ ] Audio is clean (no background noise, consistent volume)
- [ ] Captions are accurate and styled per platform
- [ ] Thumbnail is scroll-stopping
- [ ] CTA is clear and aligned with business goal
- [ ] No screenshots or low-quality visuals
- [ ] Duration is appropriate for platform

## 6. Quality Standards Per Brand

### Visual Identity

| Brand | Color Palette | Energy Level | Font Style | Thumbnail Style |
|-------|--------------|-------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Jon (Personal)** | Dark navy, white, gold accent | High energy, confident | Bold sans-serif | Face close-up + bold text overlay |
| **AbsolutePay** | Electric blue, white, dark grey | Bold, disruptive | Modern geometric | Product UI + benefit statement |
| **Biptap** | Deep green, white, silver | Professional, steady | Clean sans-serif | Infrastructure visual + data point |
| **1TX0** | Black, neon green, white | Future-forward, technical | Mono/tech font | Abstract/web3 visual + bold claim |
| **BSQ** | Purple, cream, gold | Premium, creative | Elegant serif + sans | Portfolio showcase + client name |

### Caption Standards Per Platform

| Platform | Style | Font | Position |
|----------|-------|------|----------|
| **LinkedIn** | Clean, white with shadow, 2-3 words per line | Bold sans-serif | Bottom center |
| **IG Reels** | Animated pop-in, colored keywords | Trending CapCut style | Center |
| **TikTok** | Native TikTok captions (auto) or CapCut animated | Platform native | Center-bottom |
| **YouTube** | Burned-in + SRT upload (dual approach) | Standard readable | Bottom |

### Audio Standards
- Minimum: 44.1kHz, no background noise
- Voice should be 6dB above ambient
- Background music (if any): -20dB under voice
- Descript Studio Sound feature: ON for all talking head content

## 7. Distribution Workflow

```
Video approved → Export per platform:
├── LinkedIn: 16:9 or 1:1, native upload, captions burned in
├── YouTube: 16:9, full resolution, SRT captions, custom thumbnail
├── IG Reels: 9:16, under 90s, trending audio optional
├── TikTok: 9:16, under 60s preferred, native captions
└── X/Twitter: 16:9 or 1:1, under 140s, auto-play optimized
```

**Upload Checklist:**
- [ ] Platform-specific aspect ratio
- [ ] Captions embedded (not just SRT)
- [ ] Thumbnail uploaded (YouTube, LinkedIn)
- [ ] First comment drafted (engagement bait)
- [ ] Hashtags (max 3 LinkedIn, max 5 IG, trending on TikTok)
- [ ] Scheduled for optimal time (check Luna's analytics for best posting windows)
