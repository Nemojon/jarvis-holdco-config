# Carousel Content Pillars — v2

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
Affluent entrepreneurs, investors, founders. People with money. High-net-worth individuals who value:
- Unfair advantages and insider knowledge
- Premium lifestyle and intentional living
- Personal transformation and abundance mindset
- AI and technology as leverage

## Aesthetic
Premium. Black and gold. Editorial quality. Think Vogue meets TechCrunch.
NO generic stock photos. NO corporate clip art. NO basic templates.

## Pillar 1: AI Tools & Productivity
**Tone:** Authoritative, insider knowledge
**Angle:** "Here's the unfair advantage"
**Topics:** AI tools, automations, systems, workflows, productivity hacks for founders
**Examples:** "5 AI tools billionaires use", "The morning stack that runs my empire", "AI replaced my $10K/mo team"

## Pillar 2: Entrepreneurship
**Tone:** Bold, experienced, no-BS
**Angle:** "What separates the 1% from the rest"
**Topics:** Building, scaling, hard truths, revenue, hiring, firing, pivots
**Examples:** "I went from $0 to $10M ARR", "The lie about work-life balance", "Why most founders fail by year 3"

## Pillar 3: Transformation & Manifestation
**Tone:** Aspirational, spiritual but grounded, premium
**Angle:** Law of Attraction, abundance mindset, intentional living, energy alignment
**Topics:** Morning routines, visualization, gratitude, energetic alignment, identity shifts
**Examples:** "The morning routine that attracted my first million", "Rich people don't grind — they align", "I manifested this life. Here's how."

## Crossover Ideas
- AI + Manifestation: "I used AI to design my dream life"
- Entrepreneurship + Transformation: "The identity shift that 10x'd my revenue"
- AI + Entrepreneurship: "The AI stack that runs a $10M business with 3 people"
